Concentrated / Dilute Binodal Data ！ Separated
================================================

For each system, THREE files:
  binodal_{system}_concentrated.csv  ！ concentrated phase (donor-rich)
  binodal_{system}_dilute.csv        ！ dilute phase (acceptor-rich)
  spinodal_{system}.csv              ！ spinodal curve
  critical_{system}.csv              ！ critical point

Columns in binodal files:
  phi1_L8Bo = Acceptor volume fraction
  phi2_Solvent = Solvent volume fraction
  phi3_Donor = Donor volume fraction

In Origin ternary plot: X=phi1, Y=phi3, Z=phi2
